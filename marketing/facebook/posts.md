## Posting rules that keep the page safe

- Do not post or boost anything that promises a result: "bring back your ex", "win your case", "double your money", "guaranteed".
- Avoid lottery, betting and gambling luck entirely on Facebook. Meta restricts gambling content and it draws reports.
- Do not use before-and-after testimonials with outcomes. If you share client words, keep them about how they felt treated.
- No health claims: never "cure", "treat" or "heal" a named condition. "Wellbeing", "rest", "balance" are fine.
- Keep the 18+ line on every post and reply to comments warmly without diagnosing or promising anything in public. Move real conversations to WhatsApp.
- Boost only posts 01, 02, 03, 05 and 07. Posts about spells are fine organically but are the ones most often rejected as paid ads.
- Respond to every message within the stated hours. Pages that go quiet after an ad get reported as scams.
